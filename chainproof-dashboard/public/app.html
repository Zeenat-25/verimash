THE PROBLEM

How can Chain B trust an event on Chain A?

Two separate blockchains do not automatically know what happened on the other. VeriMash demonstrates a verifiable cross-chain message workflow: inspect the source event, gather validator signatures, enforce approval on the destination, then record an audit receipt.

What the demo proves

Chain A produces an on-chain message event; the existing backend validates its receipt and bytes, gathers two test-validator approvals and relays delivery to Chain B. An on-chain destination gate enforces approval.

What it does not claim

This prototype uses a trusted local relay, RPC nodes, and locally controlled validator keys. It is a message-verification demonstration, not a production decentralized bridge or a transfer of cryptocurrency.
