01 / THE PROBLEM

Two blockchains.
Two separate versions of truth.

A source-chain transaction doesn't automatically prove anything to a destination chain. Without the right checks, messages can be replayed, altered, or delivered without authorization.

The challenge isn't just sending information. It's making the destination verify what actually happened.

01Was the source event real?
02Who approved the message?
03Can the destination safely accept it?
02 / OUR APPROACH

Don't assume.
Verify the entire path.

VeriMash turns a cross-chain message into an inspectable journey: source transaction evidence, test-validator approvals, destination-side enforcement, and a recorded audit receipt.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

From event to evidence.

One clear flow. Every meaningful step can be inspected in the live application.

01

Dispatch on Chain A

A transaction emits a message event and a source-chain receipt.

02

Verify the evidence

The relay checks the transaction, dispatch event, and message identity.

03

Enforce approval

At least two of three locally controlled test validators must approve the message.

04

Record on Chain B

The destination contract enforces the check and records the delivery audit.
